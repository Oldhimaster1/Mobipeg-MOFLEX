Real encoding.
Clear expectations.

MobiPEG Web Studio runs the modified V15 FFmpeg/x264 MobiClip encoder locally through WebAssembly. Your video is never sent to a conversion server. The site downloads its own code and engine; selected media stays on your device.

Getting started

  1. Select a video and preview it. Browser preview support and encoder input support can differ.
  2. Choose 2D, full-SBS or half-SBS to match the source. SBS conversion preserves existing stereo; it does not invent depth from 2D.
  3. Choose resolution, frame rate and quality. Start with a short clip to measure this device.
  4. Keep this page visible until encoding and verification finish, then download your MOFLEX.

Queue & storage estimate

Select several videos to build a conversion queue with individual settings, ordering and removal controls. Queue order and settings are saved locally. After a reload, reselect the original videos or their folder to reconnect the saved items; the queue does not store copies of your source videos. Videos encode one at a time. Choose manual downloads, automatic saving to a folder where supported, or saving in this browser. Browser-saved outputs remain available after a reload and can be added directly to the CIA Movie Builder. Download anything you want to keep before clearing site storage.

The displayed output range and peak temporary-storage requirement are planning estimates, not guarantees. Source motion, detail, audio and advanced settings can change the final size. Available browser storage is shown when the browser provides it; your normal downloads folder is separate.

Output preview & framing

Choose a timestamp under Output preview and select Generate preview. The browser renders a framed source image, encodes one MobiClip frame (one pair of coded eyes for stereo), then decodes it for comparison. Choose Left or Right to inspect each eye. This is a video-only I-frame sample, so it does not demonstrate motion prediction, temporal filtering over time, complete-file integrity, or hardware playback. It uses the selected profile quantizer; size-limited conversions may select another value. Preview samples do not increment conversion statistics.

Framing & crop offers Fit (letterbox), Fill (center crop), and Stretch. Manual margins use even source-pixel counts and are applied to each eye after splitting SBS, before aspect correction and scaling. All margins must leave a nonempty picture; incompatible input crops fail without producing an output. Changes invalidate the preview. Reset framing clears margins and restores Fit.

Resolution options range from 256×144 to 800×480 per eye, with 10–60 fps and fractional NTSC rates. Existing defaults are unchanged. Resolutions above 400×240 and display rates above 30 fps are experimental for physical 3DS playback; higher settings increase runtime, memory and output size.

Speed, crop ratios & interface languages

Letterbox / Fit keeps the whole cropped picture and adds borders; Fill center-crops to cover the output frame. Crop aspect ratio adds 4:3, 16:9, 16:10, 5:3, 21:9, square and portrait presets. These are applied after manual margins, separately to each eye, accounting for pixel aspect ratio. Fit or Fill then places that picture into the selected output resolution.

Encoding speed defaults to Original. Fast uses less subpel motion search at the same quantizer; output pixels and file sizes can change. Advanced subme values override this switch. Full video/audio verification remains enabled. Long-movie chapters also reuse the initial input inspection instead of repeating it every chapter. See the small-fixture benchmark for measurements and limitations; these are not full-movie or iPhone speed guarantees.

The header language selector offers English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean and Simplified Chinese. It follows a supported browser language initially and remembers your selection locally. Interface translations are bundled with the site and do not contact a translation service. Audio, technical diagnostics and this guide retain their original language. Filenames and encoder settings are never translated.

Advanced parameters

Open Advanced and enable Parameter overrides for direct V15 controls: -me_method, -g, MOBI_SUBME, skip and motion-refinement thresholds, scaler flags and input range, hqdn3d coefficients, and audio gain/highpass/lowpass filters. Blank fields inherit the current profile. The invocation preview shows the generated arguments and environment; these are not an arbitrary FFmpeg shell. Stereo mapping, pixel format, output codec and movie audio alignment remain managed by MobiPEG.

Custom hqdn3d coefficients replace Image treatment. Subme 1 is rejected because it desynchronizes the custom codec. Expensive search settings can substantially increase encoding time. Turning Parameter overrides off ignores its values; Reset clears them. The conversion report includes active overrides. These settings have software decode checks, not physical 3DS certification.

Stereo & sound

Layout 1 encodes left then right at twice the displayed frame rate. At 24 stereo pairs per second, there are 48 coded eye frames per second. Half-SBS aspect correction happens before scaling each eye. Audio is aligned to the video at 48 kHz, using ADPCM by default or optional FastAudio. FastAudio is smaller but remains unverified on physical 3DS hardware; compatibility checks may fall back to ADPCM. Choose the source audio track, apply a signed audio offset if needed, or normalize its peak. Complete decoding checks audio duration and alignment; this is not a new hardware playback claim.

Animated previews

Enable Motion preview for an encoded-and-decoded clip of up to 2.5 seconds. Play, pause, scrub and compare the stereo eyes before encoding the whole video. Preview resolution and frame rate are bounded to limit memory use. A still or animated preview does not replace complete-file verification.

HDR to SDR

Automatic detects tagged PQ/HDR10 and HLG sources and maps them from BT.2020 to BT.709 SDR with a locally generated color lookup table. Use Source transfer for HDR files without reliable tags. Mobius, Hable and Reinhard offer different highlight treatments. Automatic leaves SDR sources unchanged. HDR processing runs locally with both engines.

Scene-aware size allocation

Size-targeted movie jobs can sample texture and motion in each range, reserve audio space and allocate more available video bytes to difficult ranges. Completed ranges return unused budget to queued work; active encoders retain their reservations. Uniform uses the previous time-based allocation. The measured benefit is better automatic use of the requested cap; equal-actual-size quality tied a manually retuned uniform baseline. Source sampling is bounded rather than exhaustive, and gains vary by video.

MOFLEX playback, seeking and SUPER MOFLEX

Open a local file in the MOFLEX Player to play it, select stereo eyes or export MP4/MKV. Supported indexed movies can seek directly to validated decoding starts; older or unsupported files can still require slower forward decoding. Indexed seek tests cover generated 30–120-minute movies, exact audio/video output and stereo ordering. Browser measurements do not prove physical 3DS seeking behavior.

The SUPER MOFLEX editor can update movie metadata and artwork, import subtitles and alternate audio, and adjust subtitle timing. Save the edited file to retain changes. Export and playback choices depend on the selected tracks and output format; SUPER extras require a compatible reader and are not a promise of support in the original Nintendo player.

CIA Movie Builder

The CIA Movie Builder creates real installable CIA files locally, not only project JSON. You can choose your own compatible, decrypted player-template CIA. The optional VidInjector setting downloads the upstream player files when you build, rather than bundling a template with MobiPEG. Check that you have permission to use those files; their redistribution terms are unclear. A compatible collection-player template supports 1–27 movies with custom episode names and ordering; a single-movie player template supports one movie. Choose a HOME Menu title, publisher and unused title ID, plus icons, episode thumbnails and banners. PNG, BMP and JPG artwork is converted locally using the compatible template banner model; prepared CBMD banners are also accepted. Optional banner audio accepts PCM16 WAV, mono or stereo at 8–48 kHz, up to 10 seconds.

Save project JSON to retain episode order, names and settings. Reopen it with its referenced movies and artwork. Choose your player CIA again if you use your own template. Project JSON does not embed your videos. Completed CIAs can be downloaded or saved in browser storage where quota permits; saved downloads can be reopened after a reload.

Final MOFLEX and CIA files must fit FAT32: at most 4,294,967,295 bytes per file, including packaging overhead. Enable automatic collection splitting to produce several CIAs in episode order when the collection is too large. An individual oversized episode still needs to be reduced; it cannot be split into playable pieces by the CIA packager. Check that the generated title IDs do not conflict with installed applications. Existing Azahar playback tests cover the player; this release does not add physical 3DS verification.

Offline use and updates

Choose Save app for offline use to cache the interface and both engines. Offline conversion, playback and CIA building remain local and still need their source files and any player template. The release manifest checks cached asset hashes. When an update is ready, close all MobiPEG tabs or installed-app windows and reopen the app to activate it. Browser cache and storage quotas or eviction can remove offline data; keep separate copies of important outputs and projects.

Browser limitations

The published web release has automated conversion, playback, packaging and offline checks; physical iPhone and Nintendo 3DS testing remain separate. WebAssembly threads require cross-origin isolation, supplied by server headers or the site’s service-worker fallback on supported browsers. Automatic selects SIMD where available; Compatibility selects the scalar build. Each encoder uses memory for its current range. Long Movie Mode stores verified ranges privately on-device and assembles the final file without holding the entire movie output in JavaScript memory. More workers, larger ranges and high-resolution inputs still increase memory use. Storage quota, eviction and device limits can interrupt a job; a successful desktop conversion does not establish an iPhone memory limit.

Keep the screen on and the tab visible during conversion. A wake lock is requested where available, but mobile systems can still suspend or terminate tabs. Interrupted Long Movie Mode jobs can reuse verified, checksummed ranges stored privately in this browser. Reselect the same original video with the same settings to resume; unfinished or damaged ranges are encoded again. Saved movie recoveries lists available jobs and lets you delete them. This is checkpoint recovery, not a pause button or an exact-frame continuation. Short single-range jobs restart from the beginning. Browser storage must be available and retained; eviction, private browsing or clearing site data can remove recovery files. Progress estimates apply to the current stage.

Verification

Verified outputs pass complete video/audio decoding and coded frame-count checks. If verification cannot finish, any offered result is clearly marked unverified. This does not certify the Nintendo player's seeking, stereo presentation, or hardware playback. Full test results, source, build instructions and engine provenance are included with the project download.

Privacy & usage statistics

Privacy: videos and filenames stay on your device. Unless you opt out, we send Cloudflare a page-view count and, after a verified conversion, its duration and input/output sizes. Only daily totals are stored; no cookies or visitor IDs. Cloudflare processes connection metadata, including your IP address. Browser Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are respected.

Statistics describe page views, verified conversions, converted duration and file sizes. They do not identify unique people. Use the checkbox at the bottom of the studio to opt out. This preference is stored locally; interface-language preferences are also stored locally. Neither setting is a visitor identifier.
